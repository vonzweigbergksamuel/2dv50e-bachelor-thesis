One important factor is that _DeepFace_ experiment was conducted with _verifaction_. Which means that the program got two images of the same person and then had to decide if they were the same person or not. We want to test the _identification_ task, which means that the program gets one image and then has to identify the person in the image from a database of known people.

We also discussed the possibility of measuring the performance of the models with a timer, to see how long it takes for the model to identify the person in the image.
